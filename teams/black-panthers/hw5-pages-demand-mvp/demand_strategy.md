# Plandit Demand Strategy

## Traffic owners

**Hunter Doradea and Catherine Escobar** own traffic generation, community outreach, publishing, and weekly channel reporting. The rest of the team will support distribution through personal accounts and campus networks.

We will begin with organic outreach and will not run paid ads yet.

## Initial communities

### 1. r/nyu

NYU students live in New York City and describe having many options but struggling to turn them into plans.

> "I think because there is SO MUCH cool stuff to do everyday, the amount of options feels overwhelming."

### 2. r/AskNYC

People in this community want to enjoy the city without overspending. Plandit's budget, time, and distance constraints directly address that need.

> "I'm broke broke right now lol, but I don't want to just sit at home all day."

### 3. r/columbia

Columbia students have limited free time and need realistic plans that fit around schoolwork.

> "You'll be doing your homework most weekends."

These communities and quotes came from the team's Reddit research. Posts will follow each community's rules, lead with a useful question or insight, and avoid spam.

## Additional organic channels

- Ask campus creators and local micro-influencers to make and share short videos demonstrating a saved post becoming a plan.
- Partner with student clubs whose members regularly organize social activities.
- Add a referral link that rewards both the inviter and invited friend after the MVP can reliably track referrals.

## Blog post

**Planned title:** *Why Your Saved Places Never Become Plans*

The post will explain the gap between saving an interesting place and actually going. It will cover the four constraints found in our research: too many scattered saves, limited budgets, travel-time limits, and group indecision. The call to action will link to:

[https://www.plandit.app/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=owned&utm_campaign=hw5_launch&utm_content=saved_places_post](https://www.plandit.app/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=owned&utm_campaign=hw5_launch&utm_content=saved_places_post)

## Measurement

Google Analytics 4 will measure:

- Landing-page visitors
- Traffic source and campaign
- Visitor-to-waitlist conversion rate
- Total waitlist signups
- Signups by channel and post

The primary metric is:

**Visitor-to-waitlist conversion rate = email signups / landing-page visitors x 100**

The team will review results weekly, compare the lowercase UTM links, and adjust channel effort and messaging based on which sources generate qualified signups.
