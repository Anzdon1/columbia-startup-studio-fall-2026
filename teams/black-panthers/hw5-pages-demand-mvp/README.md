The submission includes both the main site at `plandit.app` and a separate class preview. The class preview is **for preview purposes only** and has three distinct pages: **Homepage**, **Make Plans**, and **Share Plans**. Its page structure, messaging, and visual direction informed the more final landing-page experience now published at `plandit.app`.

The primary `plandit.app` call to action is **Get early access**, and its email signup is connected to the live waitlist form. The class preview demonstrates the earlier three-page concept, but its waitlist is preview-only and does not save emails.
