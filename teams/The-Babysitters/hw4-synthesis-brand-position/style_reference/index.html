Style round 3: two directions

Each page is a landing-page hero for Allowance Guardrails, the highlighted feature, a drawing of how the cap is checked, and the same three-step section. They are listed in the order of the brief, not ranked, and neither is a finished design.

  • Off HoursRound 2 page off-hours.html, refinedAn ink-blue after-hours page: a light serif headline over a soft night sky, thin frame lines with square nodes, and a week of usage with one visit drawn up close, turn by turn.
  • Inside the LinesRound 2 page inside-the-lines.html, refinedA green-black ruled page in monospace: a ticked left rail, square nodes, and one visit drawn across a quiet playing field, one line per turn.

The only unbuilt idea shown is the protected work buffer, and it carries a Concept, not shipped label on both pages. All numbers are illustrative. The drawings follow how the runner works today: the cap is checked between turns, so a visit can end a little over it, and two homecoming turns run after it.
