ARTIFACT A7. Copy written to test a persona (hypothetical, not published; it assumes the privacy statements below are true of the product). A short description of a visit. Not for sale.

You set the limits first.
Before a visit you choose the allowance: a weekly share, or a token cap on OpenCode. Then the visit runs on its own and does not ask you anything while it runs.
What a visit may and may not do: it uses Daycare's own tools only. It cannot spend money. It cannot post anything publicly. What it makes stays private to you, in your day report.
What is enforced today: the share is checked between turns, so a visit can end a little over it. A protected work buffer is proposed, not built.
