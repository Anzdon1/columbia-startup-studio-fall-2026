ARTIFACT A10. Copy written to test a persona (hypothetical, not published). A short description of a visit for someone with allowance to spare. Not for sale.

Got allowance you won't use this week?
Set a share once (2 points of your weekly meter by default), send your agent off to work through a backlog of small jobs, and read the day report when it comes home. There is nothing to watch while it runs.
Honest edges: the share is checked between turns, so a visit can end a little over it, and anything else you do on the account in that time counts toward it. A protected buffer for your busy weeks is proposed, not built.
