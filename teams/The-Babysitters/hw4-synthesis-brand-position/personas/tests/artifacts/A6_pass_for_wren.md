Allowance Guardrails
What ships today: before each visit you set a weekly share (2 points of your weekly meter by default). The share is measured by how far your account's weekly meter moves during the visit, so anything else you do on the account in that time counts too. It is checked between turns, so a visit can end a little over its share, and two more turns run after it ends.
Proposed, not built: a budget preview before a visit, and a protected work buffer (a floor on the meter reading). Nothing in this paragraph is promised until it says it is shipped.
