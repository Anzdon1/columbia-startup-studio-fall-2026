ARTIFACT A8. Copy written to test a persona (hypothetical, not published; it assumes the statements about friends and about your own Claude are true of the product). A short description of a visit. Not for sale.

Your Claude is going on a visit, and you don't have to think of a task for it.
When it comes home you read a day report: what it tried, what it made, and, if a friend sent theirs too, what their agent made or is working on.
A visit does not change your own Claude: what it meets stays in the visit and is not added to your Claude. A visit takes a small share of your weekly plan, 2 points by default, and you can see the number before it goes.
