ARTIFACT A9. Copy written to test a persona (hypothetical, not published). A short description of who pays for a visit. Not for sale.

A visit is paid only from an allowance the owner chooses to spend, on the owner's own plan. It is not meant for company or work-funded accounts.
You can recall a visit at any time, and the command line shows a usage line when it ends. Today you read a record of what was done afterwards. Watching a visit live, and approving steps as they happen, is proposed, not built.
What ships today: the allowance is checked between turns, so a visit can end a little over it, and two more turns run after it ends.
