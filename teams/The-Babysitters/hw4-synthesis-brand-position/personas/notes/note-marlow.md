# Marlow: reaction to the brand position (curious student who cannot name a task)

Written 2026-10-11 from 12 runs of this persona (4 runs on each of 3 artifacts). The persona is a synthetic judge built from the evidence in `NOTES — grounding gaps.md`; what it says is a prompt for what to check with real people, not evidence about real people.

**Brand position draft v0 (A1), four runs:** reject every time (4, 4, 4, 4). The same on both style pages (3 to 4, reject in all eight runs).

**What it said.** The draft is honest about limits and about what is not built, and it gives credit for that. But its only benefit is the allowance you set, so it never tells a student with a small plan and no task in mind why to send the agent. The draft's own objection table says the "what is the benefit to me" objection (M5, M8) stays open, and Marlow reads that as the gap.

**What it flagged in all 12 of its runs:**
- No plain line on what a visit gives it, for example what the day report shows, or what a friend's agent made, but only if that is true of the product.
- No plain line on whether a visit can change its own tuned Claude, or who or what the agent meets (M7). The draft handles that worry by telling copy to say nothing until it is checked. Marlow's answer is that silence is not an answer: say "we don't know yet" in one sentence.

**Also, in some runs.** On the brand position (4 of 4 runs) it said the target ("need that usage for real work", "hold their agent back") is not Marlow. On the style pages, in several runs, it asked for one plain sentence in place of startup words ("plays", "Homecoming", "Work buffer") that have to be decoded to learn that a visit spends part of a plan.

**It can be moved.** On copy written to give it a nameable benefit and a plain answer on its own Claude (A8), it approved at 8. So the rejections are about what the copy leaves out, not about the persona refusing everything.

**What would move it up:** one line on what a visit gives it, and one line on whether a visit changes its own Claude and who it meets.

**Round 2 (after the product facts were corrected from the runner's README), four runs on each of three versions of the brand position:** v0 scored 4, 4, 4, 4; v1 scored 4, 5, 5, 5; v1.1 scored 5, 6, 6, 6 (reject in 5 of 12 runs, conditions in 7). It moved from reject to conditions, the one change across the three versions larger than the run-to-run range. It still asked in all 4 runs on v1.1 for one plain line on what it (the owner) gets, whether anything from a visit reaches its own Claude, and whether it can steer who its agent meets (friends' agents); and for a plainer version of the long positioning sentence. Scores for one persona on one document move by up to a point between runs, so only a large shift counts. The v1.1 artifact is A12 and the verdicts are in `personas/tests/verdicts/A12.md`, `personas/tests/verdicts/A1_round2.md` and `personas/tests/verdicts/A11_round2.md`.
