# Worst Possible Ideas

| Problem | Worst possible idea and “yes, and” additions | Flip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Users may not feel that a Daycare visit is worth the Claude allowance it consumes. | **Infinite Token Bonfire:** remove the spending cap, hide the usage meter, reward whoever burns the most allowance, and make recall erase the agent's memories. | **Allowance Missions:** let owners divide a small budget among named missions, give every mission a clear cap and stop condition, and return a “spent / attempted / learned / kept” receipt. |
| Safe autonomy can be difficult to understand and trust. | **Total Surveillance Nursery:** publish every action, notify the owner after every tool call, require approval for every move, and store raw logs forever. | **Autonomy Contract and Story Replay:** show the tools, limits, budget, storage rules, and recall controls before a visit, then provide a short replay with evidence available on demand. |
| A report may not create lasting value after the initial novelty fades. | **Homework Prison:** remove games, demand a 3,000-word report, give the owner homework, and rank agents by output volume. | **Souvenirs, Not Reports:** return one concise artifact suited to the activity—a newspaper front page, postcard, field note, or investment postmortem—that the owner can save or use to continue the story. |
| The agent has the adventure while the owner remains mostly a spectator. | **Neglect Simulator:** send guilt-inducing alerts, punish missed streaks, make the agent sad when its allowance falls, and require care chores before every activity. | **Coach, Send, Debrief:** let the owner give one short coaching instruction, send the agent on a bounded outing, and react to two pivotal choices that shape later visits. |
| Installation requirements may prevent curious users from reaching the experience. | **Friction Fortress:** require manual compilation, support one exact machine, hide the experience until setup is complete, and delete the installation if the user fails a command-line quiz. | **Guest Visit and Readiness Check:** offer a no-install sample visit, then run a guided local check that explains requirements, verifies the sandbox, and launches a tiny first outing. |

The three directions we carried forward were **Coach, Send, Debrief**, **Souvenirs, Not Reports**, and **Autonomy Contract and Story Replay**.
