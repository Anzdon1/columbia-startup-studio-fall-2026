## Record format and limitations

The team supplied Chinese notes from completed interviews. Each record preserves that text and includes an English translation. The translated passages and key quotes are not original English utterances. The notes do not retain a complete question-by-question transcript, so we cannot establish whether each problem was raised without prompting.

The interview guides were structured during preparation of this submission. Exact original question wording was not retained; the guides include prompts for subsequent interviews and are not claimed to be the verbatim scripts used.

The snapshots include all twelve fields from Class 3 slides 67–69. Original emotional-intensity ratings and snapshot completion times were not recorded in the material available for this submission. These fields remain explicitly marked as missing, and we cannot establish that the one-hour snapshot requirement was met. Interview dates and interviewer names were also not provided in the source notes.

Our student sample does not fully match our original target customers: paying web studios and owners of recurring document-processing pipelines. In particular, CR03, ST01 and ST05 have limited or no current need for the original workflow. These interviews help identify boundaries and adjacent problems, but should not be treated as five qualified buyers per idea.
