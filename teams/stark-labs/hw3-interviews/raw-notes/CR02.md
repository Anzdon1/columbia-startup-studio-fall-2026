# CR02 ClientReady interview notes

**Participant:** Student who assembled a course-project website  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

上学期我们小组给一个课程项目做了简单的网站，我主要负责把大家写的东西放上去。最麻烦的不是收不到内容，是有人写得特别长，有人就写两句话，放在一起很不统一。

最后我们在提交前一起改了一晚上，不过也包括改网站，具体整理内容用了多久我记不清了。我们就是用 Google Docs，没有额外花钱。如果工具能告诉大家每一部分大概需要写什么，可能有用。但其实我们四个人在一个文档里也能完成，不知道会不会专门换工具。你们可以问问我组员。

## English translation

Last semester, our group made a simple website for a course project. I mostly put everyone's writing on the site. The biggest problem wasn't getting the content. It was that one person wrote a lot and someone else wrote two sentences, so the sections didn't feel consistent.

We spent an evening fixing things together before the deadline, but that included work on the website too. I don't remember how much of it was specifically content editing. We used Google Docs and didn't pay for anything extra. A tool that tells everyone what each section needs might help. But the four of us could also do that in one document, so I'm not sure we'd switch tools just for this. You could ask my teammates.
