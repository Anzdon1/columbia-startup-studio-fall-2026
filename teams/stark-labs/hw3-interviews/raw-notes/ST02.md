# ST02 Strata interview notes

**Participant:** Student manually collecting report data for a group assignment  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

有一次小组作业要从一些报告里整理数据，我们把数字填到一个共享表格里。我负责其中几份。PDF 本身都挺清楚，比较麻烦的是报告的年份、单位不一样，有时候还会看错表格那一列。

我们试过复制表格，但格式乱掉了，最后还是手动录入，再互相检查。具体多花了多久不好说，大概一个下午，不全是复制的问题。没有花钱。如果工具能直接提取表格，我愿意试，但我还是会核对数字。比起便宜一点，我更在意它会不会给我一个看起来很对、其实错了的结果。

## English translation

For one group assignment, we had to collect data from reports and put the numbers into a shared spreadsheet. I handled a few of the reports. The PDFs themselves were clear. The harder part was that the years and units differed, and sometimes we read the wrong table column.

We tried copying tables, but the formatting got messed up. We ended up entering the data manually and checking each other's work. It's hard to say how much extra time it took. We spent roughly an afternoon, but copying wasn't the only issue. We didn't spend any money. I'd be willing to try a tool that extracts tables, but I'd still check the numbers. I'm more concerned about getting something that looks correct but is actually wrong than saving a little money.
