# CR03 ClientReady interview notes

**Participant:** Student building a personal portfolio website  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

我做过自己的作品集，但没有太多向别人收材料的情况。只有一个小组项目需要问队友要截图，发消息之后他当天就给我了。

真正花时间的是我自己不知道该怎么介绍项目，一直改文字和排版。所以，如果这个工具只是帮我收材料，我可能不太需要。如果能通过几个问题帮我把项目经历写清楚，我会想试一下。网站相关的费用有，但不是为了收集内容花的。身边有同学也在做作品集，不过不确定他们有没有这个问题。

## English translation

I've made my own portfolio, but I haven't really needed to collect much from other people. For one group project, I asked a teammate for screenshots, and he sent them the same day.

What actually took time was figuring out how to explain my projects. I kept changing the text and layout. So if the tool only helps me collect material, I probably don't need it. If it could ask a few questions and help me explain my project experience more clearly, I'd be interested in trying that. I've spent money on the website, but not on collecting content. Some classmates are making portfolios too, although I don't know if they have this problem.
