# CR04 ClientReady interview notes

**Participant:** Student who maintained an event registration page  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

我们做过一个活动报名页面，算不算网站我也不太确定。主要就是活动介绍、时间地点，再放一个报名链接。我负责把信息放上去。

当时比较烦的是地点后来换了，我得改页面，还要确认群里发出去的信息是不是也改了。这个不是材料没收齐，是信息一直在变。我们没买什么工具，平时也是谁有空谁改。你说的按页面整理可能有帮助，但我们的内容很少，我更希望能清楚知道哪个是最新版本。愿意回访，但可能得避开期中考试那周。

## English translation

We made an event registration page, although I'm not sure whether that counts as a website. It mainly had an event description, the time and location, and a registration link. I was responsible for putting the information up.

The annoying part was that the location changed later. I had to update the page and check whether the information posted in the group chat had been updated too. It wasn't that we were missing material. The information kept changing. We didn't buy any tools, and whoever had time would make the updates. Organizing content by page might help, but we had very little content. I'd rather be able to tell clearly which version is current. I'd be open to a follow-up, but probably not during midterm week.
