# ClientReady interview guide

This is a proposed structured guide prepared when organizing the interview materials. Exact original questions were not retained in the supplied notes, so this document is not presented as the verbatim script used in the completed conversations.

Target: people who have personally collected content from others for a website. Record whether this was paid client work, a student project, or a personal favor.

## Opening (about 2 minutes)

Hi, we're students working on a class project at Columbia. We'd like to understand your own experience with this workflow. This should take about 20–25 minutes. Honest feedback, including that something is not a problem for you, is useful. Is it okay if we take notes?

If recording is desired, ask for permission separately before starting.

## Background (2–3 minutes)

1. What kinds of websites have you worked on, and what was your role?
2. Tell me about the most recent one that required other people to provide content.

## Problem exploration (5–7 minutes)

3. Walk me through how you got the text, images, and information for that project.
4. What was hardest, if anything? Tell me about a specific time it happened.
5. Why was that difficult, and what happened next?
6. What was the impact on your time, the schedule, or payment? Which numbers are estimates?
7. How often has this happened? Tell me about a case that went smoothly, too.

## Current solutions (3–4 minutes)

8. What have you tried to handle this? What worked and what did not?
9. Have you paid for anything specifically to address it? How much and for what?

## Solution signal (3–4 minutes; introduce the idea only now)

We're exploring ClientReady. It would ask contributors short questions based on the website's pages, organize answers and existing materials by page, and show what's still missing.
10. Have you tried anything similar before? Why did you keep or stop using it?
11. What's your initial reaction?
12. In the situation you described, what might it help with and what would remain difficult?
13. What concerns would you have?

## Wrap-up (about 2 minutes)

14. If you could change one thing about this process, what would it be?
15. What should I have asked but didn't?
16. Do you know one or two other people with relevant experience? Would you connect us?
17. Would you be open to a ten-minute follow-up in a couple of weeks?
