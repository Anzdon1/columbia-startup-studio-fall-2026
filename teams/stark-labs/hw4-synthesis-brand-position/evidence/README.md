# Evidence provenance

The team supplied ten Chinese interview records and confirmed that they came from real interviews. On October 9, a subsequent reflection called them “simulated answers”; when asked about that conflict, the user explicitly clarified that this was a typo and the records were real interviews. This package follows that clarified account. No recordings were provided for independent verification.

`raw-notes/` and `snapshots/` preserve the files prepared for Homework 3. Chinese records are the source language; English passages are translations. The snapshots were reconstructed from the supplied records, not demonstrated to have been completed within one hour. Missing original questions, interview dates, emotion ratings and timing remain missing.

`team_reflection_zh.md` preserves the subsequent team-provided interpretation verbatim, including the original typo, with the later clarification noted above. It was supplied after earlier AI assistance. Its inclusion does not satisfy the assignment's requirement that Part A be completed before opening an AI.

`quote_audit.json` records exact matches for the five Part B quotes and hashes of all twenty source files. Exact matching verifies correspondence with the supplied written notes, not whether those notes are verbatim audio transcripts.

All 12 first-round Reddit search listings were manually saved and parsed (9 nonempty, 3 zero-result listings). Twenty of 21 selected comment-thread snapshots have been read, with 54 verified quotation records. The expected R2-09 save remains missing because its file duplicates R2-08; see ../reddit_research/coverage.md. Persona verdicts are synthetic artifact reviews, not interviews or new market evidence.
