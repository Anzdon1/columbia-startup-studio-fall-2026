# ST03 Strata interview notes

**Participant:** Student research assistant assigned scanned-material extraction tasks  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

我这学期帮老师做一点研究助理的工作，其中有一部分是看扫描材料，把需要的信息记到表格里。我没有负责整个处理流程，就是分到一些文件去整理。

有些扫描件很清楚，有些比较糊，软件识别出来以后还是得对照原图看。最花时间的也是这些模糊的地方。软件是不是学校付费的我不清楚，我自己没付钱。如果你的工具能把不确定的地方标出来，可能比直接给我一大段文字更有用。但材料能不能传到外部工具，我得先问负责项目的人。回访可以，原始文件不一定能分享。

## English translation

I'm doing some research-assistant work for a professor this semester. Part of it involves reading scanned material and recording the information we need in a spreadsheet. I don't manage the whole workflow. I just get assigned some files to work through.

Some scans are clear and others are blurry. Even after the software recognizes the text, I still have to compare it with the original image. The blurry parts take the most time. I don't know whether the university pays for the software. I haven't paid for it myself. A tool that flags uncertain parts might be more useful than one that just gives me a long block of text. But I'd need to ask the person running the project before uploading the material to an external tool. A follow-up is fine, but I may not be able to share the original files.
