# ST01 Strata interview notes

**Participant:** Student reading PDFs for a term paper  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

我写期末论文的时候会看很多 PDF，但不是把整篇都提取出来，主要是找相关段落，记到自己的文档里。大部分可以直接复制，偶尔从扫描版里复制不了，我就自己打几个句子。

有点烦，但还没有烦到让我专门找一个工具。我也没有为这个付过钱。如果工具能保留页码，我会觉得比较有用，因为写引用的时候还得回去找。你说降低成本，我自己没太大感觉，我现在用的基本都是免费的。可以回访，不过我可能不是特别典型的用户。

## English translation

I read a lot of PDFs when I'm writing a term paper, but I don't extract the whole document. I mostly find relevant passages and put them in my notes. Usually I can copy the text. Occasionally I can't copy from a scanned version, so I type a few sentences myself.

It's a little annoying, but not enough that I've looked for a dedicated tool. I haven't paid for this either. Keeping the page numbers would be useful, because I have to go back and find them when I'm writing citations. Lowering costs doesn't mean much to me personally because almost everything I use is free. I'd be open to a follow-up, although I might not be a very typical user.
