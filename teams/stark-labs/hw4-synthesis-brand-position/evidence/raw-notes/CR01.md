# CR01 ClientReady interview notes

**Participant:** Student who updated an event page for a student organization  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

我不是从头做网站，就是帮一个学生组织更新活动页面。上次要放嘉宾介绍和照片，材料基本都是群里发给我的。有个人一直没发简介，我就先空着，后来再补。整理其实没多久，主要是等人，断断续续拖了几天。

我们用共享文档，没花钱。有活动才会弄。你说的工具可能方便我看还缺什么，但如果对方不回消息，好像还是得我去催。我不太想让所有人再注册一个账号。可以找我们另一个负责活动的同学聊，我也可以再聊一次。

## English translation

I didn't build the site from scratch. I helped a student organization update an event page. Last time, we needed speaker bios and photos, and most of the material came through our group chat. One person still hadn't sent their bio, so I left that part blank and added it later. Organizing everything didn't take that long. It was mostly waiting for people, which stretched over a few days.

We used a shared document and didn't spend any money. We only do this when there's an event. Your tool might make it easier to see what's missing, but if someone doesn't reply, I think I'd still have to chase them. I wouldn't really want everyone to register for another account. You could talk to another student who helps organize our events. I'd also be open to talking again.
