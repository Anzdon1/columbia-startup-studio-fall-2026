# CR05 ClientReady interview notes

**Participant:** Student who built a project website as an unpaid favor for a friend  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

我帮朋友做过一个介绍他们项目的网站，没有收钱，就是帮忙。他一开始说内容都准备好了，但后来给我的其实是一个很长的文档，还有一堆图片，我要自己判断哪些放首页、哪些不用。

最后我们打了两次电话才弄清楚。我觉得多花了几个小时吧，没有认真记。没有试过专门的工具。你这个如果能先让他把内容分好，我会轻松一点，但我感觉很多时候他自己也没想清楚要说什么。所以工具可能能帮忙提问，但最后还是要一起讨论。我可以先问问他愿不愿意接受访谈。

## English translation

I helped a friend make a website about their project. I didn't charge anything; it was just a favor. He initially said the content was ready, but what he sent was one long document and a bunch of images. I had to work out what belonged on the homepage and what we didn't need.

We ended up having two calls to sort it out. I think it added a few hours, but I didn't track it carefully. I haven't tried any dedicated tools. If your tool could help him sort the content first, that would make things easier for me. But I think a lot of the time he wasn't sure what he wanted to say either. The tool might help by asking questions, but we'd probably still need to discuss it together. I can ask him whether he'd be willing to be interviewed.
