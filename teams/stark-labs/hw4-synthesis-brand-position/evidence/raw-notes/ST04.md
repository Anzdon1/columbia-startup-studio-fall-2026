# ST04 Strata interview notes

**Participant:** Student responsible for PDF handling in a programming course project  
**Interview date and interviewer:** Not recorded in the supplied notes.

These are the original Chinese notes supplied by the team, followed by an English translation. They are not presented as a verbatim English transcript. Exact original questions were not retained.

## Original Chinese notes

我们有个课程项目需要读取 PDF 的内容，我负责文件处理这部分。开始拿几份能选中文字的 PDF 测试都正常，后来换成扫描件，才发现提取出来是空的。

后来加了 OCR，配置和调试花了一些时间，差不多一个周末里断断续续弄的。我们没有认真算成本，因为测试文件不多，用的也是免费的工具。你说的先简单提取、不行再用别的方法，我们其实也想过。要不要用现成的，主要看接起来是否方便、效果是不是比我们自己做的好。可以找我们组里另一个同学聊，他负责后面的检索。

## English translation

We had a course project that needed to read PDF content, and I handled the file-processing part. It worked with the first few PDFs, where the text was selectable. Then we tried scanned files and found that the extracted output was empty.

We added OCR afterward. Setup and debugging took some time, on and off over a weekend. We didn't really calculate costs because we had few test files and used free tools. We had also thought about trying simple extraction first and using another method if that didn't work. Whether we'd use an existing tool would depend on how easy it is to integrate and whether it works better than what we built. You could talk to another teammate who handled the retrieval part.
