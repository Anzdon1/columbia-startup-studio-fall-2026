# Persona agents and actual synthetic tests

Three course-template personas are installed in the product repository. Each preserves two exact Chinese interview excerpts and now has two linked, dated Reddit context excerpts. Names are fictional aliases; unrecorded ages and demographics remain unknown. Ratings are synthetic calibration settings. Reddit context does not change a student persona into a validated professional buyer.

## Current test — after the Reddit revision

On October 9, three separate agents received their own frozen persona and the same [brand v2](tests/brand_position_v2.md). Their exact JSON responses, input hashes and contract checks are retained. The internal brand already summarized earlier v1 reviews; this is not a blinded experiment. No desired verdict or score was requested and no other v2 response was supplied.

That final small revision has not been retested; the scores above belong only to frozen v2.
