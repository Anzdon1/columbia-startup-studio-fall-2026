## Concept

Two offset pages move into alignment. An open rear-page outline sits behind a solid foreground page with two content lines. The mark is drawn on a 64-unit grid; transparent cutouts preserve the line detail across light, dark and one-color variants. The small favicon uses optical adjustments on a 16-unit grid.

## Use

- Primary colors: terracotta `#943E23`, ink `#4E281F`, cream `#F8ECD9`.
- On dark: apricot `#E8B890` symbol and cream wordmark. Use only as a deliberate dark treatment.
- Horizontal lockup: the visible mark is 32 units high, matching the wordmark capitals. Top and baseline align; keep the 20-unit gap.
- Clearspace: at least 16 units around the 64-unit mark (one quarter of its height).
- Minimum size: 16 px for the special favicon; 24 px for the full mark; 100 px width for a wordmark; 130 px for a lockup. Prefer larger whenever space allows.
- Never stretch, rotate, add gradients, add a shadow to the logo, place it over a busy image, or use the full lockup in a tiny browser tab.
- SVG wordmarks use Arial/Helvetica bold, with editable text. They are portable across common systems but not outlined; if a printing vendor needs outlines, convert text to paths in the vendor workflow and check letter spacing. The symbol requires no font.
