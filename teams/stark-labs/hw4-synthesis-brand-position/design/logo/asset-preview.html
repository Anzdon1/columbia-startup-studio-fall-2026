Small-size rule: 16, 32 and 48 px PNGs use an optically simplified mark with integer-aligned edges. The 180, 192 and 512 px versions use a white mark on terracotta. Keep at least one quarter of the mark height clear around the symbol. Use a wordmark at 100 px or wider.
