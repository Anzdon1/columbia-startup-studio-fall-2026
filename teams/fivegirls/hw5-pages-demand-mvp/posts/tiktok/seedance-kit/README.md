## Finish the video

Generated screen text can change despite a prompt. For exact UI, use the included images as still inserts, or place an actual screen recording over the phone in the editor. Add the unchanged logo and final domain as overlays if the generated version distorts them. Keep the map attribution visible. Do not stretch the screenshots into a different aspect ratio.

[voiceover.txt](voiceover.txt) contains the exact narration. [captions.srt](captions.srt) is a 20-second caption draft; adjust timing to the final spoken audio. [caption.md](caption.md) contains the post copy and campaign URL. Image sources, credits, capture details, and permitted changes are in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

No older generated illustration or earlier rendered video is used as an input in this kit. The photos provide visual context, not evidence that a specific restroom is currently open or usable. The local materials are ready for production; no Seedance upload or TikTok publication has been performed.
