## Overview

This repository contains the early-stage design work for **AIDI – International Student Application Assistant Platform**. The project aims to help international students find reliable and personalized information when applying to U.S. undergraduate programs.

## Design Process

The team generated and improved possible solutions through the **Worst Possible Idea**, **AI-assisted idea expansion**, **dot voting**, and **Quick Lean Canvas** exercises.

## Results

The team selected two ideas for further research:

1. **Personalized Fit-Based School List**  
   Recommends universities based on the student’s academic profile, budget, intended major, location, and personal preferences.

2. **School Profile Snapshot**  
   Presents important university information, such as admissions requirements, costs, majors, deadlines, and international student support, in a clear and organized format.
