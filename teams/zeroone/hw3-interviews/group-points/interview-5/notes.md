* ## **Problem interview**

* Aviv Pinto: What do you organize with friends?  
* Lucas: Monthly fitness challenges with local points. We use a group chat.  
* Aviv Pinto: Tell me about the last challenge.  
* Lucas: Four of us agreed to exercise three times a week for a month. Halfway through, we disagreed about whether a long walk counted.  
* Aviv Pinto: How were sessions recorded?  
* Lucas: People posted updates when they remembered. I counted them at the end of each week, and some sessions were reported late.  
* Aviv Pinto: What was the consequence?  
* Lucas: The challenge lost momentum. With incomplete records and unclear rules, we could not finalize the leaderboard.  
* Aviv Pinto: What tools have you tried?  
* Lucas: Group messages and occasional calendar reminders. I have not bought a challenge management app.

* ## **Concept reaction**

* Interviewer: A shared space for friend-group predictions and challenges using only local points or tokens. Members confirm rules and participants; a manager records results; scoring and group standings update automatically.  
* Aviv Pinto: What part of the local-points concept would help?  
* Lucas: Clear rules everyone confirms before starting, a participant list, and enough visibility to keep the challenge going after the first few days.  
* Aviv Pinto: Who should judge a session?  
* Lucas: For subjective outcomes, participants should confirm rather than leave everything to one manager.  
* Aviv Pinto: What would make you stop using it?  
* Lucas: Too many notifications or complicated proof requirements. I want it to support the challenge without taking over.
