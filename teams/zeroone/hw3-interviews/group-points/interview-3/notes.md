* # **Group Points Interview 3**

* Interview notes  
* Interviewer: Shiyu Zhou (sz3420)  
* Record ID: GP-03  
* Interviewee: Ethan

* ## **Problem interview**

* Shiyu Zhou: What do you organize?  
* Ethan: A weekly football watch party with six friends. We plan in WhatsApp.  
* Shiyu Zhou: Tell me about the last round of predictions.  
* Ethan: We picked winners of four games and agreed on ten local points per correct pick. Everyone sent picks at different times, and two people messaged me privately.  
* Shiyu Zhou: How did you keep score?  
* Ethan: I copied the picks into a phone note. Someone changed a pick in the chat without pointing it out, which made the record unclear.  
* Shiyu Zhou: What happened at the next gathering?  
* Ethan: We had to reconstruct which picks counted before updating the scores. I enjoy doing the picks, but I do not enjoy remembering every message.  
* Shiyu Zhou: How often does that happen, and what do you spend?  
* Ethan: We do this most weekends during the season. I use WhatsApp and a phone note and do not pay for a tracking tool.

* ## **Concept reaction**

* Interviewer: A shared space for friend-group predictions and challenges using only local points or tokens. Members confirm rules and participants; a manager records results; scoring and group standings update automatically.  
* Shiyu Zhou: Which part of a shared local-points app would help?  
* Ethan: If everyone entered their own pick before a clear cutoff, it would save me work. I would want the rules, results, and standings in one place.  
* Shiyu Zhou: How should a result be recorded?  
* Ethan: The manager could enter an objective game result, but everyone should see it and be able to flag an error.  
* Shiyu Zhou: What would prevent adoption?  
* Ethan: Long setup for every member. Joining through a simple link would make trying a weekly round much easier.
