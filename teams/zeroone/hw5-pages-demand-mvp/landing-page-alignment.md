# Landing-page value propositions

The homepage and three audience pages build on the [HW4 brand position](https://github.com/et2921/CallIt/blob/90e6cfd/homework/hw-4/brand_position.md) and [interview synthesis](https://github.com/et2921/CallIt/blob/90e6cfd/homework/hw-4/synthesis_comparison.md).

| Page | Audience and main value | HW4 basis |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Groups that want a shared record of predictions and results. | Brand thesis: "Being right is better when your group remembers." |
| `/for/friends` | Friend groups that want to revisit who called an outcome. | Shared history and clear winning conditions; the synthesis distinguishes groups that care about following through from casual jokes. |
| `/for/sports` | Recurring sports groups whose organizer collects picks from chat messages. | GP-03 describes copying picks and reconstructing which answers counted. Members entering their own answers addresses this work. |
| `/for/agreements` | Groups making predictions that stay open for weeks: keep the original agreement clear. | GP-02 forgot an agreed term; GP-04 remembered different versions of a winning condition and saw value in a record for longer predictions. |

Each page leads with one benefit: shared memory, easier coordination, or clear terms. These are interpretations of HW4, not separately validated propositions.

The copy describes the current private-group flow: fixed terms, one answer per person, and a manager-recorded result that is final in this version. The scoreboard counts correct answers and answered calls for resolved predictions. Reminders and settlement tools are identified as future features.

The research supports testing these benefits. It does not establish adoption, retention, or a guarantee that groups will agree on a result. The next validation step is a real group completing a call and deciding whether to make another.

The previous `/for/teams` URL redirects permanently to `/for/agreements`, preserving query parameters for shared links and attribution.
